How to score a visit

Each number is one visit, scored out of 20. Rate four things from 0 to 5 and add them up:

  • Food: taste, freshness and how well the dishes are cooked
  • Service: friendliness and how quickly the food arrives
  • Cleanliness: tables, kitchen view and restrooms
  • Value: is the portion and quality worth the price?

0 = poor, 1 = weak, 2 = okay, 3 = good, 4 = very good, 5 = excellent

Grades: A is 18 to 20, B is 15 to 17, C is below 15. Example: food 5 + service 4 + cleanliness 5 + value 4 = 18.

To make Best quality, five visits need to total more than 90, which means averaging about 18.
